Add !clip to Your Stream

StreamClips Hub lets your viewers clip your YouTube livestream by typing !clip in chat. Setup takes just a few minutes with Nightbot and works for any channel.

1

Sign in with your YouTube channel

Log in to StreamClips Hub with the Google account that owns your YouTube channel so clips are linked directly to your dashboard.

2

Connect Nightbot & add the command

Follow the guided steps in your popover Setup Guide menu to add the command to your Nightbot chat setup.

Connect Your Kick Account

Kick has clipping built natively. Simply connect your profile and clip on Kick like normal.

1

Connect Kick channel profile

Navigate to your dashboard profile and input your Kick channel handle to sync public clips.

Downloading with yt-dlp

Using yt-dlp is the most reliable way to download raw, high-quality stream segments directly to your local editing folders.

CMD 
winget install yt-dlp.yt-dlp

Setup Guide

Add Clip commands to your Stream

Simply copy the command below and add it to your channel chat via Nightbot to let your moderators and viewers save timestamps instantly:

!addcom !clip $(urlfetch https://yt-timestamp-central-auth-project.onrender.com/api/clip?streamerId=$(channelid)&chatid=$(request_id)&text=$(querystring)&user=$(user)&userlevel=$(userlevel)&channelId=$(userid))
