Add Clip commands to your Stream

Simply copy the command below and add it to your channel chat via Nightbot to let your moderators and viewers save timestamps instantly:

!addcom !clip $(urlfetch https://yt-timestamp-central-auth-project.onrender.com/api/clip?streamerId=$(channelid)&chatid=$(request_id)&text=$(querystring)&user=$(user)&userlevel=$(userlevel)&channelId=$(userid))
